Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how EverBook ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information when you use the standalone distribution of the App. We respect your privacy and design the App to work entirely offline whenever possible. The App is open-source software distributed under the GPL-3.0 license, so anyone can audit the source code.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

* We do not require you to create an account.
* We do not collect or upload personal identifiers such as name, email, address or contact list.
* All books, reading progress and settings remain inside your device storage and are never copied to our servers.

2. Information Collected Automatically

The standalone App does not include Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Play Billing, or other Google service SDKs.

We do not receive crash reports, analytics events, Firebase installation IDs, advertising identifiers, or push-notification tokens from this distribution.

3. Optional Network Connections

The App can access the Internet only in the cases listed below:

  • Checking for App updates on GitHub if you enable "Check for updates" in Settings
  • Opening external links you tap (e.g. Help articles or project homepage)
  • Downloading cover images through the built-in image loader (coil-compose)

No other remote calls are performed.

4. Permissions Explained

  • Storage / File Access - to let you pick local ebook files and keep reading progress.
  • Post Notifications - optional; used for local update reminders.
  • Internet - used only for optional update checks, external links, and image loading you request.

5. Third-Party Open-Source Libraries

The App relies on the following open-source libraries, all of which run on-device unless you request network content such as external links or images:

  • AndroidX Jetpack Compose & Material 3 - UI framework
  • Room & DataStore - local storage
  • kotlinx.coroutines & kotlinx.serialization
  • PDFBox-Android & jsoup - file parsers
  • Coil - image loading
  • compose-reorderable - drag-and-drop
  • LazyColumnScrollbar
  • AboutLibraries
  • commonmark-java

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored data (books, settings, reading history) stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Because this distribution does not send analytics or crash reports to us, there is no server-side App data for us to retain or delete.

7. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, local App data is processed on your device only as necessary to provide the reader features you request. We do not maintain user-identifiable App data on our servers for this distribution.

8. Your Rights

You can erase or rectify local data at any time by deleting books inside the App, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling it. If you make a Data Subject Access Request, we will normally answer that we have no data relating to you from the standalone distribution.

9. International Data Transfers

The standalone distribution does not transfer analytics, crash reports, or push-notification data to Google Firebase or to our servers.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Your Consent

By checking the "I accept the privacy policy" box in the App you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. New versions will be published within the App and on the project GitHub page. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact

For questions or concerns, please open an issue on our GitHub repository, visit our website at byteflipper.com, or email us at byteflipper.business@gmail.com.
