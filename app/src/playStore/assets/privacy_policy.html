Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EverBook (“the App”, “we”, “our”) handles your information when you use the application published on Google Play. We respect your privacy and design the App to work entirely offline whenever possible. The App is open-source software distributed under the GPL-3.0 license, so anyone can audit the source code.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

• We do not require you to create an account.
• We do not collect or upload personal identifiers such as name, email, address or contact list.
• All books, reading progress and settings remain inside your device storage and are never copied to our servers.

2. Information Collected Automatically

The Google Play version of the App includes Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase In-App Messaging, and Firebase Remote Config. These services may automatically collect limited diagnostic, analytics, and app-instance information, for example:

  • Crash stack traces and device model (Crashlytics)
  • App interactions, app launches, screen views, and other analytics events (Firebase Analytics)
  • Device information such as app version, operating system version, language, country/region, and device model
  • Firebase-generated installation identifiers and related app-instance identifiers
  • Push notification delivery tokens if you allow notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

Firebase Analytics helps us understand which parts of the App are used the most, measure app stability, and improve the App. Crashlytics helps us detect and fix critical crashes. Firebase Remote Config lets the App download configuration values, such as feature flags, without requiring an app update. These services do not collect the content of your ebooks or any text you read. Google may aggregate this information in accordance with its Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The Google Play version of the App may display advertising. Ads may be enabled, disabled, or adjusted using Firebase Remote Config. When ads are shown, the App uses Google AdMob mediation. The advertising partners included in this version are Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and Yandex Mobile Ads. An ad request may be routed to one or more of these partners to select, serve, measure, or limit an advertisement.

Advertising services may collect and use information such as the Android Advertising ID, device information, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic data. This information may be used to serve, limit, measure, and improve ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal requirements.

Depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. In regions where it is required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent before personalized ads are shown or before ad-related data is used for personalization. Personalized ads may use previously collected or historical data to make ads more relevant. You can manage ad personalization and reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in your device's Google privacy or ads settings. Google handles advertising data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and partner data policy. Unity Ads and Yandex Mobile Ads may also process advertising identifiers, device and network information, approximate location, ad interactions, and consent signals for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and, where permitted, personalization. Their privacy policies are available at Unity Privacy Policy and Yandex Privacy Policy.

Google does not automatically pass your consent choice to every mediation partner. After the UMP form is completed or changed, the App forwards the applicable GDPR consent signal to Unity Ads. You can reopen the privacy options form from Settings > Privacy where it is available.

We do not share the content of your ebooks, notes, reading progress, or local library with advertising partners.

4. Remote Configuration

Firebase Remote Config is used to fetch app configuration values from Firebase, for example whether a feature such as advertising is currently enabled. Remote Config values are not secret and may be visible to a user of the App. We do not store confidential personal information in Remote Config keys or values.

5. Optional Network Connections

The App can access the Internet only in the cases listed below:

  • Checking for App updates on GitHub if you enable "Check for updates" in Settings
  • Opening external links you tap (e.g. Help articles or project homepage)
  • Downloading cover images through the built-in image loader (coil-compose)
  • Downloading on-device translation language models through Google ML Kit if you use in-app offline translation
  • Translating selected text or full books with Google Translate online if you choose that translation provider
  • Receiving push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (if you allow them)
  • Fetching Firebase Remote Config values
  • Loading and measuring ads when advertising is enabled

In-app offline translation in the Google Play version uses Google ML Kit on-device translation. Selected text and full-book text translated with ML Kit are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers. The App may download language models from Google before local translation can run. You can choose whether these model downloads require Wi-Fi in Reader translation settings.

If you choose Google Translate as the translation provider, the selected text or full-book text you translate is sent to Google for online translation. Google handles that text in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can use ML Kit offline translation or an external translator app instead where available.

6. Permissions Explained

  • Storage / File Access – to let you pick local ebook files and keep reading progress.
  • Post Notifications – optional; used for update reminders or FCM push messages.
  • Internet – required by Firebase SDKs, ads, Google Translate requests you choose, and to open external links you request.

7. Third-Party Open-Source Libraries

The App relies on the following open-source libraries. These libraries work with local App data unless you request network content such as external links, cover images, model downloads, online translation, Firebase services, or ads:

  • AndroidX Jetpack Compose & Material 3 — UI framework
  • Room & DataStore — local storage
  • kotlinx.coroutines & kotlinx.serialization
  • Google ML Kit on-device translation — local translation and language identification in the Google Play version
  • PDFBox-Android & jsoup — file parsers
  • Coil — image loading
  • compose-reorderable — drag-and-drop
  • LazyColumnScrollbar
  • AboutLibraries
  • commonmark-java

Online services such as Firebase, Google advertising services, ML Kit model hosting, and Google Translate are handled according to their own privacy terms when the related feature is used.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

• Locally stored data (books, settings, reading history) stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
• Crash and analytics logs on Firebase are retained according to Firebase and Google Analytics retention settings and then deleted or aggregated.
• Advertising data may be handled by Google, Unity, Yandex, and their advertising partners according to their own retention policies and your device or consent settings.

9. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, our legal basis for collecting and using personal information described above depends on the specific context in which we collect it. We process your information on the basis of:

  • Legitimate interest — to keep the App secure and stable (crash reports).
  • Consent — optional analytics, push notifications, and personalized ads are processed only where required consent has been obtained.
  • Legitimate interest or consent — non-personalized ads, fraud prevention, frequency capping, and ad measurement may be processed depending on local law and your consent choices.

10. Your Rights

The GDPR grants data-subjects extensive rights. However, we do not maintain our own user account database or user-identifiable app servers. Most information (books, settings, reading history) is stored only on your device; limited diagnostic, analytics, advertising, and messaging data may be held by Google/Firebase and may be associated with app installation, app instance, or advertising identifiers.

Consequently, if you make a Data Subject Access Request we will normally answer that we have no data relating to you. If you would still like to exercise your rights you can:

  • Erase or rectify local data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Withdraw or change consent by using the App's consent controls where available, disabling analytics or notifications in system settings, or changing Google privacy and ads settings on your device.
  • Reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in your device's Google privacy or ads settings.
  • Contact Google, Unity, or Yandex directly regarding data handled by their respective advertising or analytics services.

Should you send us a GDPR request, please note that without additional information we may be unable to link Firebase, Google Analytics, or advertising records to a specific natural person.

11. International Data Transfers

Firebase, Google Analytics, and advertising services may store data on servers located in the United States or other countries. Google LLC participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and standard contractual clauses are in place to safeguard such transfers.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
