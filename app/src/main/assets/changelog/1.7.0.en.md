# Notes and highlights

- Select a passage in the original text to open the annotation menu
- Choose a color from the palette to highlight an important thought, add a bookmark, or write a note for the passage
- Open “Annotations” in the reader to see all saved items and filter bookmarks, highlights, or notes
- Tap the annotation you need to return to its place in the book; you can edit or delete it

---

# Auto-scroll

- Tap anywhere on the screen to show the top and bottom bars, then tap “Auto-scroll” on the bottom bar to open the speed controls
- Use the panel to slow down or speed up scrolling, choose a speed from 1× to 10×, and pause reading when needed
- After a few seconds, the panel collapses into a compact chip so it does not cover the text; tap the chip to expand the speed controls again
- In the reader settings, you can choose the chip’s position and transparency and add a pause button for quickly stopping auto-scroll

---

# Brightness gesture

- Open the reader settings and enable “Custom brightness” to activate the gesture
- While reading, swipe vertically near the left or right edge of the screen: up for brighter, down for dimmer
- The gesture works only at the edges, leaving the center of the screen free for reading, selecting text, and regular scrolling
- Disable custom brightness in settings to return to the system brightness level

---

# Custom fonts

- Import TTF and OTF files and use them in the reader
- After importing, choose the font you need from the list
- Remove fonts from the list when you no longer need them
